# NoWatermark logo concepts

Six square, raster logo explorations generated with the built-in image-generation workflow. Each PNG is 1254 × 1254 on the approved warm-paper ground (`#fffefa`), using near-black ink (`#121115`) and restrained stamp red (`#c4271a`). The briefs required a monochrome-first construction, heavy forms, sharp geometry, and no security or surveillance clichés.

1. `01-halftone-hidden-n.png` — A field of weighted halftone dots makes the mark feel like information resolving only under closer inspection. **Weakest point:** at favicon size the dot field becomes a dense abstract pattern and the intended letter is not reliably legible.
2. `02-panel-gutter-n.png` — Four heavy panels and their white gutter form an unmistakable N, directly echoing the site’s comic-panel system. **Weakest point:** its panel symmetry is somewhat conventional, and the red upper-right panel has to remain optional for a strict one-colour lockup.
3. `03-interlocking-nw.png` — An interlocking N/W construction uses oversized cropped planes to make a compact, assertive monogram. **Weakest point:** the overlapping geometry is visually busier than the strongest candidates and the W is less immediate than the N.
4. `04-redaction-knockout.png` — A positive diagonal knockout turns a redaction bar into an N, connecting the mark to the product’s honest treatment of withheld or removed information. **Weakest point:** the horizontal format has less square-icon presence than the panel marks and can read as a generic edit mark without context.
5. `05-registration-frame.png` — Thick print-registration brackets frame a decisive negative-space N, grounding the identity in image and print production rather than security symbolism. **Weakest point:** the central open frame can suggest a crop/focus symbol if its proportions are not maintained carefully.
6. `06-modular-ink-blot.png` — A modular black ink mass with a stepped knockout embodies an intentionally marked unknown while staying bold and technical. **Weakest point:** it is the most abstract option; the central N cue is subtle and the loose surrounding blocks are not ideal at tiny sizes.

## Recommendation

**Lead with `02-panel-gutter-n.png`.** It is the clearest direct translation of the approved panel-grid language, reads as an N immediately, and uses large, durable shapes that survive a favicon. It also has the cleanest path to a one-colour vector redraw.

**Keep `05-registration-frame.png` as the second finalist.** Its print-production reference fits an image-metadata product elegantly, while the negative-space N makes it distinct from generic privacy marks. It will need a small optical refinement to prevent a focus-reticle reading.

## Favicon note

`02-panel-gutter-n.png` is the safest at 32px, followed by `05-registration-frame.png` if the brackets stay thick. `04-redaction-knockout.png` should remain recognizable but is more dependent on context. `01-halftone-hidden-n.png`, `03-interlocking-nw.png`, and `06-modular-ink-blot.png` are better used at larger sizes or simplified before favicon use.

## Generation record

Built-in image generation was used for six separately prompted `logo-brand` concepts: halftone hidden N; panel-gutter N; interlocking NW; redaction-bar N knockout; print-registration frame N; and modular ink-blot N. Every prompt fixed the approved palette, paper background, square framing, flat opaque geometry, thick favicon-safe forms, and the exclusions for Japanese characters/cultural iconography, security clichés, magnifiers, locks, shields, eyes, AI sparkles, gradients, and thin hairlines.
